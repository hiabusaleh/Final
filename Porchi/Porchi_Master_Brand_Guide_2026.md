# PORCHI — Master Brand Guide

**Version:** 1.0 · **Date:** 24 September 2026 · **Status:** Strategic foundation for approval  
**Master line:** **Porchi — স্বপ্ন পূরণের জন্য পড়ছি।**  
**Current launch:** IELTS preparation · **Designed to expand:** English learning, academic learning, career readiness and learner services.

---

## 1. Executive decision

Porchi should not enter the market as another provider promising an IELTS band score. That territory is crowded: established providers already sell live classes, mocks, feedback, teacher credibility and large content libraries. Porchi's defensible space is different:

> **A progress-led education brand that understands why a learner is studying, shows what to do next, and makes real improvement visible.**

IELTS is the first proof of this system, not the permanent definition of the company. The Bengali sound-association **“পড়ছি”** gives the brand a rare long-term asset: it is a living verb, a habit, and an identity. It can naturally travel from IELTS to English, school, university, skills and career learning without a rename.

### The Porchi equation

`Personal dream + clear next step + deliberate practice + useful feedback + visible progress = lasting learner trust`

### Non-negotiable brand position

**For ambitious Bangladeshi learners who want to move forward, Porchi turns a large future goal into a clear, supportive and measurable daily learning journey. Unlike course marketplaces or score-claim coaching centres, Porchi combines human teaching with a structured practice and progress system.**

This is a positioning choice, not a claim that must be repeated word-for-word in public.

## 2. What the research says

### Market reality

- The official IELTS journey is built around four skills, targeted practice, feedback and test familiarity. Academic and General Training share Listening and Speaking, but use different Reading and Writing modules. That makes clear diagnostics and correct course routing essential—not optional. [IELTS test format](https://www.ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test) · [official sample questions](https://www.ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test)
- 10 Minute School currently competes with large quantities of live teaching, partial/full mocks, individual progress tracking, speaking practice and feedback. [10MS IELTS Programme](https://10minuteschool.com/en/product/ielts-programme/)
- Mentors' competes with regular, executive and crash formats, substantial mock volume, live feedback and established instructor credentials. [Mentors' IELTS course](https://mentors.com.bd/courses/ielts) · [current fees](https://mentors.com.bd/fees)
- S@ifur's already stretches from IELTS into spoken English, grammar, kids' English, school programmes and career education. This proves the expansion path is strategically normal, but it also means Porchi must protect one coherent master brand. [S@ifur's course portfolio](https://saifurs.com.bd/about/page/courses)

### The white space

The category is saturated with **features**. It is much less consistent at making a learner feel:

1. “This is made for my reason for learning.”
2. “I know exactly what I should do today.”
3. “I can see why I am improving—or not improving.”
4. “If I pause, I can return without shame.”

Porchi already has evidence for this white space in its learning materials: a deep Writing platform with Learn → Practice → Analyse, an AI Teacher, Error Bank, Cambridge practice, vocabulary review, 15-minute daily routine, audio learning, handwriting-to-text support, and a 53-skill mastery course. The content asset is unusually deep; the brand should surface the **system**, not drown prospective learners in file counts or feature lists.

### Strategic implication

Lead with **progress and clarity**. Prove it through the product. Use scores and success stories as verified evidence, never as a shortcut to trust.

## 4. Brand architecture: grow without losing meaning

### Recommended architecture: one master brand, descriptive products

```
PORCHI
├── Porchi IELTS                    [launch category]
│   ├── IELTS Foundation             [English/grammar readiness]
│   ├── IELTS Academic Prep
│   ├── IELTS General Training Prep
│   ├── Writing Lab
│   ├── Speaking Studio
│   ├── Mock & Feedback
│   └── Daily Practice
├── Porchi English                  [next adjacency]
│   ├── Spoken English
│   ├── Grammar & Writing
│   └── Vocabulary & Pronunciation
├── Porchi School                   [future]
├── Porchi Skills                   [future]
└── Porchi Career / Study Pathways  [future; launch only with qualified advice]
```

### Architecture rules

1. **Porchi always comes first.** Write `Porchi IELTS`, never invent a detached name such as “BandMaster”.
2. The product descriptor explains the job; it does not compete with the master brand for attention.
3. Use **Lab**, **Studio**, **Practice**, **Foundation** and **Prep** only when the experience earns the name.
4. A new category must pass three tests: it advances a learner's future, can offer a structured path, and can show useful progress.
5. Do not launch study-abroad counselling, visa support or admissions services under Porchi until qualified processes, disclaimers, privacy controls and partner due diligence exist. Education guidance and immigration advice are not the same promise.

### Expansion sequence

| Horizon | Focus | Brand job |
|---|---|---|
| 0–12 months | IELTS + English foundations | Prove the Porchi learning system and earn results. |
| 12–24 months | Spoken English, grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation | Become useful before, during and after IELTS. |
| 24–48 months | School English, academic writing, career communication | Own lifelong English and learning progress. |
| Later | Wider academic/skills products | Expand only where the Porchi system creates a real advantage. |

## 5. Audience, jobs and journeys

### Primary audiences at launch

| Segment | Real job they need done | Barrier | Porchi answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Study-abroad aspirant, 18–28 | Reach a required band with a plan. | Doesn't know starting level or what to study first. | Diagnostic → target route → daily plan → feedback. |
| Working professional / migration candidate | Fit preparation around a busy life. | Time pressure and fear of wasting money. | Flexible milestones, short daily actions, clear General/Academic routing. |
| Restarting learner | Recover after an earlier low score or pause. | Shame and lost confidence. | Honest baseline, weak-area repair, non-judgmental return path. |
| English-foundation learner | Build enough language confidence before test strategy. | Jumps into IELTS too early. | Porchi IELTS Foundation with a transparent readiness gate. |

### Core learner journey

`Dream → Discover → Diagnose → Plan → Learn → Practise → Receive feedback → Repair weak areas → Mock → Outcome → Share`

Every Porchi touchpoint must answer one of these three questions:

1. **আমি এখন কোথায়?** — level, target, weak areas, stage.
2. **এখন কী করব?** — one clear next action, not a giant menu.
3. **আমি কতটা এগিয়েছি?** — evidence of completed practice, improved accuracy, feedback and milestones.

### Entry offers that fit the brand

- 10–15 minute IELTS readiness check, with an honest “what this can/cannot tell you” note.
- Free writing checklist or speaking prompt pack, followed by one relevant practice action.
- A 7-day “আজ ১% এগোই” challenge.
- One diagnostic mock component—not an inflated “free band score”.

## 6. Messaging system

### Message hierarchy

| Level | Message | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Master | **Porchi — স্বপ্ন পূরণের জন্য পড়ছি।** | Footer, brand film, About, formal assets. |
| Emotional question | **তুমি কীসের জন্য পড়ছো?** | Campaigns, onboarding, stories. |
| Functional proof | **শেখা, practice, feedback আর progress—একটা পরিষ্কার পথে।** | Course pages, product demos. |
| Daily motivation | **আজও একটু এগিয়ে যাই।** | Dashboard, notifications, social. |
| Recovery | **কয়েকদিন পড়া হয়নি? সমস্যা নেই। আবার শুরু করি।** | Reactivation, support. |
| Conversion | **নিজের level যাচাই করি।** / **আজকের পড়া শুরু করি।** | CTA. |

### Copy formula

**Reason → practical action → gentle forward movement**

> “বিদেশে পড়ার স্বপ্ন আছে? আজ 15 মিনিটের Reading practice দিয়ে নিজের starting point জানি।”

Not:

> “Band 8 নিশ্চিত! এখনই ভর্তি করুন!”

### Voice

Porchi sounds **warm, clear, modern, capable, encouraging and specific**. It uses natural Bangla first; English test terms appear only where they help learning.

| Prefer | Avoid |
|---|---|
| “চলুন, শুরু করি।” | “শেষ সুযোগ! এখনই নাহলে…” |
| “এই জায়গাটায় আরও practice দরকার।” | “আপনি দুর্বল/ফেল করেছেন।” |
| “লক্ষ্য অনুযায়ী plan করি।” | “সবার জন্য এক রুটিন।” |
| “এই mockটি আপনার current performance দেখায়।” | “এটাই আপনার official band score।” |
| “ফল ব্যক্তি, সময় ও practice-এর ওপর নির্ভর করে।” | “নিশ্চিত visa / admission / scholarship।” |

### Bengali/English language rules

- Use **তুমি** as default in learner product and social communication; use **আপনি** in formal customer support where appropriate. Do not mix them in one flow.
- Write common actions in plain Bangla: `শুরু করি`, `দেখি`, `জমা দিই`, `আবার চেষ্টা করি`.
- Preserve accurate IELTS terminology: Academic, General Training, Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking, band score.
- Never call internal AI feedback an “IELTS examiner” or “official score”. Label it `Porchi AI feedback` and show its limits.

## 8. Visual identity system

No final logo file was supplied for this guide. The system below is the recommended design direction; lock it only after the actual logo, trademark search and accessibility tests are reviewed.

### Design idea

**Minimal + human + progress.** The visual feeling should be calm, focused and forward-moving—not corporate, not childish, not a generic coaching-centre poster.

### Proposed colour tokens

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Porchi Ink | `#13251D` | Primary text, dark backgrounds, authority. |
| Progress Green | `#147D67` | Primary CTA, progress, links. |
| Growth Lime | `#A8D86D` | Small highlight, completion, celebratory detail. |
| Warm Paper | `#F7F4EC` | Main soft background. |
| Mist | `#E8F1EC` | Cards, quiet states, UI grouping. |
| Signal Coral | `#E86C54` | Error or urgent attention only; never a default CTA. |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Surfaces and space. |

Use colour to explain state, not decorate everything. Body text must meet WCAG AA contrast; do not place light lime text on white or use colour alone to communicate correctness.

### Typography

The current Writing site already uses a strong multilingual stack. Standardise it:

| Use | Typeface |
|---|---|
| Bengali interface/body | Noto Sans Bengali |
| English headings | Plus Jakarta Sans |
| English UI, numbers, buttons | Inter |
| Long reading, model answers, writing area | Source Serif 4 |
| Timer, code, data | IBM Plex Mono |

Rules: use sentence case; avoid all caps in Bangla; reserve bold for hierarchy, not every phrase; write large, generous Bengali line-height; ensure mobile body text is comfortably readable.

### Logo behaviour

- Primary lockup: `Porchi` with tagline `স্বপ্ন পূরণের জন্য পড়ছি।`
- Small spaces: wordmark only; do not shrink the tagline until unreadable.
- Keep clear space around the wordmark equal to the height of the `P`.
- Never stretch, outline, shadow, rotate, recolour arbitrarily or place on a busy image without a calm container.
- Do not use an IELTS-style visual treatment or red accent that creates confusion with official IELTS branding.

### Visual motifs

Use a restrained family of: a path, a step, a highlighted word, an underline, a quiet progress bar, a checkmark, a notebook mark, and one directional arrow. Each should suggest **the next meaningful step**.

### Imagery

Prioritise real learners, real hands writing, peer practice, teachers explaining, study desks and authentic moments of relief/progress. Respect consent, especially around minor learners and scorecards. Avoid dominant use of western stock imagery, visa stamps, aeroplanes, “foreign country” clichés and staged trophy poses.

## 9. Product and website experience

### Homepage structure

1. **Hero:** `স্বপ্ন পূরণের জন্য পড়ছি।` + a concrete subtitle + `নিজের level যাচাই করি`.
2. **Reason selector:** study abroad, career, migration, confidence, personal growth.
3. **How Porchi works:** Diagnose → Plan → Learn → Practice → Feedback → Progress.
4. **Start where you are:** an honest diagnostic entry point.
5. **Product proof:** a live view of lesson, weak-area repair, feedback or dashboard—not only feature icons.
6. **Course paths:** Foundation, Academic, General Training and skill-focused offerings.
7. **Verified learner stories:** why, starting point, method, evidence, outcome.
8. **Free value:** a useful resource or practice activity.
9. **Transparent offer:** inclusions, time, support, price/refund terms and constraints.
10. **Final CTA:** `আজকের পড়া শুরু করি।`

### Product principles

- Begin every learner with a route, not a catalogue.
- Keep `Learn → Practice → Feedback → Repair → Retry` visible in the interface.
- Mark self-check, teacher feedback and AI feedback clearly as different things.
- Make Academic vs General Training choice explicit before learners enter Reading/Writing material.
- Let learners save progress, resume naturally and recover from inactivity without loss or guilt.
- Make the core daily action genuinely small: 10–20 minutes, one task, one clear completion state.

### What to foreground from the current content estate

| Asset | Public-facing Porchi value |
|---|---|
| Writing Learn → Practice → Analyse | “You do not just watch a lesson; you practise, receive a structured check and improve.” |
| Error Bank + weak-area drill | “Your mistakes can become your next practice plan.” |
| 15-minute routine + vocabulary review | “A big goal can begin with today’s 15 minutes.” |
| AI Teacher + handwriting workflow | “Get help turning practice into revision—clearly labelled as Porchi AI feedback.” |
| Cambridge practice + timed tests | “Build familiarity under realistic timing; show material provenance/licensing clearly.” |
| 53-skill mastery system | “Build the underlying English skills that make test strategy work.” |

### Important legal/content caution

The physical IELTS folder includes third-party books, Cambridge materials and other resources. Do not assume that possession gives Porchi public distribution, commercial reproduction or advertising rights. Audit ownership/licensing before publishing any book scans, branded questions, answer keys, recordings, logos or student work. When in doubt, create original practice and link to official free sample resources rather than republishing material.

## 11. Acquisition, nurture and conversion

### Funnel

`Useful content → diagnostic / free practice → personalised route → proof + support → enrolment → activation → practice completion → outcome → referral`

### Best first conversion event

**Diagnostic completion**, not “buy now.” It produces a helpful learner moment and first-party understanding of intent. Use only the data needed; explain why you collect it and obtain marketing consent separately.

### 14-day nurture outline

| Timing | Message job | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Deliver value + next step | “তোমার result-এর ভিত্তিতে এই 1টা জায়গা আগে ঠিক করি।” |
| Day 1 | Teach | One relevant skill lesson or practice prompt. |
| Day 3 | Build belief | A verified learner's starting point → method → result. |
| Day 5 | Reduce confusion | Academic vs General / course-route explainer. |
| Day 7 | Invite action | Diagnostic follow-up or free live practice. |
| Day 10 | Address barrier | Time, confidence, course fit, support FAQ. |
| Day 14 | Clear offer | Exact inclusions, price, deadline only if real, easy support route. |

### Campaign rule

Never run only “Join our IELTS course” ads. Run three creative territories in parallel:

1. **Reason-led:** “তুমি কীসের জন্য পড়ছো?”
2. **Problem-led:** “Writing-এ ঠিক কোথায় নম্বর আটকে যায়?”
3. **Proof-led:** a real learner route and credible outcome.

Test one variable at a time: hook, format, CTA, proof type or offer. Keep a learning log—not only a winners list.

## 12. Trust, evidence and safety

### Evidence hierarchy

1. Official, dated documents and verified score evidence.
2. Product demonstration and transparent curriculum.
3. Instructor credentials and sample teaching.
4. Consent-based learner testimony with context.
5. Aggregated internal data with definition, time period and methodology.

### Success-story template

`Why I started → starting point → difficulty → specific Porchi actions → changes in practice → verified outcome → honest next chapter.`

Always show the relevant context: date, test type, score breakdown if the learner consents, prior attempt if relevant, and what result does **not** prove. A single high score is evidence of one learner's outcome, not a promise for everyone.

### Privacy minimums

- Give a plain-language privacy notice before collecting diagnostic, account, payment, essay, audio or image data.
- Obtain separate consent for promotional use of learner stories.
- Apply the strictest privacy approach to learners under 18; seek guardian consent where required.
- Define retention, access and deletion procedures before accumulating writing samples and AI interactions.
- Do not train, publicly display or share learner work without explicit permission.

## 13. Measurement system

| Stage | Main metric | Quality check |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | qualified reach / completed video views | saves, shares, brand search. |
| Interest | diagnostic start rate | completion rate, not click volume alone. |
| Consideration | route/course-page engagement | webinar attendance, support questions. |
| Conversion | diagnostic-to-enrolment rate | CAC, refund rate, fit by segment. |
| Activation | first lesson + first practice completed | time to first meaningful action. |
| Learning | weekly practice, feedback use, mock participation | improvement by skill, not only logins. |
| Outcome | verified relevant result / target progress | satisfaction and learner confidence. |
| Advocacy | consented review/referral | quality and authenticity of stories. |

Do not optimise for vanity metrics. A 500-view practice video that creates 60 meaningful practice starts is strategically stronger than a 100,000-view generic motivational reel with no learner action.

## 14. Governance: how the brand stays valuable

### Required approvals

| Asset | Owner / approval |
|---|---|
| Logo, colours, typography, templates | Brand owner / designated designer. |
| Academic claims, scoring, course outcomes | Academic lead. |
| Paid ads, deadlines, pricing, offers | Commercial lead. |
| Testimonials, scorecards, learner media | Consent + brand owner. |
| AI feedback claims and data handling | Product + privacy owner. |
| IELTS/third-party material use | Legal/licensing review. |

### The Porchi pre-publish test

Before any page, post, ad, lesson or notification goes live, answer yes to all:

1. Does it sound recognisably like Porchi?
2. Does it give a learner something useful or a meaningful next step?
3. Is every claim provable today?
4. Does it support a learner without manipulation or shame?
5. Is the academic, AI or official status labelled accurately?
6. Is permission in place for every person, score, image and third-party asset?
7. Would this still make sense if Porchi expands beyond IELTS?

## 15. 90-day implementation plan

### Days 1–30 — lock the foundation

- Approve master positioning, portfolio names, tagline and voice.
- Complete logo/trademark, domain/handle and legal-content audit before public launch.
- Create the visual kit: logo variants, colour tokens, typography, social templates, landing-page components, consent templates.
- Build the public content map from the existing learning assets: Foundation, Academic, General, Writing Lab, Mock & Feedback, Daily Practice.
- Instrument core events: diagnostic start/complete, route selected, lesson completed, practice submitted, feedback viewed, enrolment.

### Days 31–60 — launch and learn

- Launch one clean homepage and one diagnostic journey before expanding pages.
- Publish 20–30 content assets across the eight series; ensure at least 40% offer practical teaching.
- Run three campaign territories (reason, problem, proof) with small test budgets.
- Record 3–5 teacher sample lessons and collect only consented first learner stories.
- Start supportive WhatsApp/email nurture with opt-in and a visible unsubscribe path.

### Days 61–90 — prove and scale

- Refine the diagnostic route from completion and learner-support data.
- Make the 15-minute routine, Error Bank and feedback loop central in activation.
- Scale only the creative and audience combinations that improve qualified diagnostic completion and enrolment.
- Publish one transparent monthly learning/progress report with definitions.
- Establish a monthly brand review: claim audit, visual audit, learner feedback, content performance and product friction.

## 16. Final direction

Porchi's long-term value will not come from owning the loudest IELTS promise. It will come from consistently owning this belief in a learner's mind:

> **“Porchi আমাকে ভয় দেখায় না। আমার কারণটা বোঝে, আমাকে পরের কাজটা দেখায়, আর প্রতিদিনের চেষ্টাকে progress-এ পরিণত করে।”**

That belief is portable. IELTS gives it a sharp first use case; disciplined execution turns it into an education brand.
